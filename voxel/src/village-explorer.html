How to explore

Drag to look around. Pinch or scroll to zoom. With two fingers, drag to pan.

Walking: left thumb moves, right thumb looks. Keyboard: WASD, Q/E to turn, Shift to jog. Controller: left stick to move, right stick to look, ✕ to interact.

Walk to a door or tap it to enter. The library, workshop and Velvet Bat welcome visitors.
