This package continues the verified `bebcb19` atmosphere/stair/journal checkpoint on the isolated `contractor/village-fidelity` branch. The user’s dusk village reference guides the palette, entrance composition, fine stonework, warm lamps and compact brass-edged plum journal. All comparison pictures come from the playable three-dimensional world with the normal renderer.

Save version remains **20**. All 77 story IDs, both authoritative quest engines, coins, rank credit, rewards, 676 resource positions, district/home gates and navigation remain compatible. The Stopped Hour proposal and further story migration remain in their existing review roadmap.

Of that, 640 boxes belong to the new foreground, whose highest point is 0.611 design units. Outside the expanded arrival footprint, the original 36,655-box geometry/colour fingerprint remains unchanged.

The existing sky dome gains layered plum clouds, rose-gold horizon light and three restrained silhouette bands. The first actual camera render exposed weak cloud coverage and excessive pink, so the procedural banks were moved into the observed low-camera cone and cooled. An original painted cloud panorama then supplies fine billow detail on the same dome: one 1024×512 WebP, 34,878 encoded bytes and a full RGBA mip-chain estimate of 2,796,204 bytes. World-direction projection and a bounded seam blend keep full camera rotation continuous. Its geometry, draw and original authored colour uniforms remain. Surface lighting reuses the district/saved-clock palette and eight-light pool, with moon ×0.84 and hemisphere ×0.9 so lantern warmth reads against dusk. Those factors do not compound; interiors and canonical atlas captures retain their authored lighting.

Stone has softer broad variation and fine filtered neutral-surface grain/mortar depth in the existing voxel-finish shader. No new material architecture or texture is needed. Emissive glass retains its previous finish. Native crown/roof visibility composes with the revised shader cache key.
