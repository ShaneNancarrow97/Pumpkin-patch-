# Roadmap after Living Mothlight

This updates the first saved-visit roadmap. It is a sequenced plan, not a claim that the whole valley is playable.

## Delivered in this contractor slice

The Green now connects gathering → friendship stories → material/coin handovers → restored exteriors → owned decorations → Elowen's reading nook → the shared supper. The journal makes the steps and the farm's cooking prerequisite visible. Four modest gathering upgrades and the repeatable almanac give the player useful reasons to return.

Explicit quest tracking, completed stories, rewards/next friendship gifts, gathering readiness, retained panel focus, a compact controller legend and two touch sticks are implemented. Elowen and Rowan now have autonomous local walking routes. Their existing art has consistent world framing and tighter menu portraits.

## Next: make the first chapter a richer place to live

1. Give the three Green interiors a small number of authored, useful interactions: library reading/research, workshop commissions and a tavern order. Reuse existing recipes/materials rather than minting another currency. Each activity should return a specific recipe, costume or furnishing to the farm.
2. Add a short cinematic shared-supper scene after Two Doorbells. Keep authored text, Skip/Back, narration fallbacks and a once-only reward claim. A skipped scene must leave the same saved outcome.
3. Add a visible route guide between active objectives. Reuse navigation obstacles and live resident positions; a camera destination is not an automatic walking path. Test gates, elevation, water and decoration changes before adding followers.
4. Add workshop commissions that consume surplus Green materials without flattening farm progression. Use one open commission per resident and bounded rewards. Simulate early/mid/late farms before changing sale income.

## Then open one new district end to end

Open **Moonroot Copse** next, following the existing district plan (planned level 6). Deliver Mabel and Tansy’s friendship state, the bramble opening, cottage homes, hardwood/moth-silk gathering, authored stories, one small puzzle and a farm-facing reward together. Supply real gates and saved quest conditions before removing the current district barriers.

Required acceptance: new players and imported saves can reach the district; puzzle state survives reload; costs are charged once; NPCs cannot walk through water or unopened routes; the reward has a clear farm purpose; all interactions work on touch, keyboard and pad.

Only after that slice is finished should the Moor's carnival, higher terraces and later residents open. Existing distant scenery and the explorer's cast preview do not constitute a playable quest district.

## Long-term progression

- Village friendship → signature recipes/costumes → farm sales and attraction appeal.
- Home furnishing and useful kitchen equipment → new dishes → tavern/visitor commissions.
- Farm earnings → restored village homes → material tools and meaningful decorating choices.
- Optional mysteries and almanac collections → premium keepsakes, with bounded coin bonuses that do not replace normal commerce.
- Attraction chapters and the village must share the existing 20-level pacing. Add unlock previews and sinks before increasing income or capacity.
