# Claude handover 00 — baseline and art boundaries

Date: 18 October 2026. Baseline: `voxel-reference` at `52853055a917e5984f6a4b28886961d5a9f0947c`.

## What is new in this build

The farm is a three.js voxel diorama, with separate room/shop places and DOM sheets. The cast are still textured sprite planes, tilted and stretched for the camera. Shane and Natalie have four shallow depth layers; visitors turn with the path. A separate optional voxel-character mode exists. The voxel save is version 2 under `pumpkin-patch-voxel-reference-v1`; it is independent of the recovered root 2D game's schema 9.

Do not replace the voxel model with the older 2D work. Root `src/core`, the voxel rules, currencies, costs, save keys, map layout and cameras are outside this art pass.

## Art delivery contract

Later commits supply source atlases, deterministic baking, frame metadata and a preview. Each actor has three idle frames and six actual walk poses with one facing direction, consistent scale and a common foot anchor. Costume heads keep Shane/Natalie's identities visible where the design permits. Extra visitor appearances must not alter customer budgets, profiles, route timing or capacity.

Planned parts:

1. Improved original Shane and Natalie.
2. Both characters in pumpkin, Monsters Inc., Toy Story, yellow sitcom and Chucky/Tiffany-style costumes.
3. Six additional 90s/00s horror-costume visitors.

Each completed part gets its own handover and commit. Preview and bake instructions accompany the assets. Do not consider a planned sheet implemented until that part's handover says it passed inspection.

Generated `voxel/assets` and metadata must be regenerated by tooling, not patched manually. New editable source images belong under `voxel/art-source/expansion`. Existing art remains available; cosmetic catalogue/rule registration is Claude's integration boundary unless a later handover explicitly says otherwise.
