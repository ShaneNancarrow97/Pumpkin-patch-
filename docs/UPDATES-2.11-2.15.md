# Historical HTML updates — 2.11 to 2.15

These describe the earlier downloadable **2D HTML** releases, as recorded in their roadmap and release notes. They are not a claim that the recovered root 2.10 source or the independent voxel reference contains every change.

## 2.11 — Preserving bench

- Orange pumpkin chutney with timed 1/5/10-batch preparation.
- Ingredients reserved during production; cancellation, reload and indoor checkout holds stay consistent.
- Pantry limits, illustrated jars and budgeted purchases from indoor displays.
- Save schema 10. Premium recipes and other produce remained planned.

## 2.12 — Shop appearance

- Previewable Moonlit apothecary with a matching interior and on-map facade.
- Original appearance remains reusable, with three purchasable fixtures for two measured spots.
- Cosmetic purchases preserve customers, stock and production; schema 11.
- Additional themes and movable shop layouts remained planned.

## 2.13 — Upgrade paths and quieter UI

- Five branches organised 32 existing purchase steps across 15 cards.
- Live costs, prerequisites, completion and a next reachable purchase, sharing existing ownership and commands.
- Compact HUD and navigation, map drag/tap isolation, Fit/Explore and resize-aware camera centring.
- Schema 11; new research effects, ratings and districts were not implemented.

## 2.14 — Visitors and traffic

- Outdoor visitors follow the stone walkway; indoor arrivals fork towards the door.
- Three extra arrival-frequency levels and two party-size levels, up to three outdoor guests per wave.
- Staggered arrivals, independent budgets and baskets, and a twelve-guest safety cap.
- Stall traffic readout and shortcut; paths expanded to 37 steps across 17 cards.
- Schema 12, preserving paid progress. Larger groups did not solve the remaining stockpile by themselves.

## 2.15 — Visual refinement

- Shared plum/amber menu styling, typography, cards, selection treatment and touch-sized close controls.
- Aligned top navigation, clearer ready/purchase/disabled states and visible small-phone/house balances.
- Fewer redundant completion labels and clearer menu hierarchy.
- Schema 12 and gameplay prices retained.
- Recorded automated desktop, phone and Fold-sized Chromium checks; physical Fold hardware and downloaded-file Safari/Chrome remained open.

## Relationship to the voxel game

The voxel reference has its own state, renderer and feature implementations. Compare feature behaviour rather than copying old release numbers or replacing voxel modules with 2D ones. The current sprite-expansion work preserves the voxel core and is delivered through the handovers in `voxel/docs/handovers/`.
